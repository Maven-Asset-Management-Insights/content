---
## Overview
IoT sensors are everywhere on the plant floor, but sensor data on its own doesn't reduce downtime or extend asset life. The value only shows up once that data is connected to Maximo's asset hierarchy, filtered for what actually matters, and routed into real maintenance action.

In this Field Kit, we outline a practical path for connecting IoT and OT data to Maximo Monitor, deciding what to instrument first, and avoiding the common trap of collecting more data without changing what technicians and planners actually do differently.

Maven Asset Management brings deep expertise in condition monitoring and IoT-to-Maximo integration, helping organizations move from "we have sensors" to "our sensors are driving better decisions."
---
## Key Business Benefits
- Detect anomalies earlier through real-time condition data instead of calendar-based inspections
- Reduce reactive work by auto-generating work orders when thresholds are crossed
- Improve trust in asset health scoring by grounding it in live sensor and OT data
- Give planners and supervisors a shared, real-time view of asset condition
- Build the data foundation needed for predictive maintenance and digital twin use cases down the road
---
## Implementation Considerations
- Start with a small set of critical assets, not a plant-wide sensor rollout
- Define what action each alert should trigger before turning it on, so alerts don't just pile up unread
- Map sensor metrics to Maximo's Meter and Asset Data Dictionary structure early, not as an afterthought
- Set thresholds with input from the people who'll act on them, not just the data source
- Plan for how IoT data will feed into existing PM strategies rather than running as a parallel process
---
## Suggested Next Steps
1. Identify 3–5 critical assets where unplanned downtime has the highest business impact
2. Confirm what sensor or OT data is already available versus what needs new instrumentation
3. Take Maven's Maximo IoT Value Assessment scorecard to benchmark where your program stands today
4. Pilot one clear alert-to-work-order workflow before scaling further
5. Establish baseline downtime and reactive-work metrics to measure improvement against
